 Li Auto Inc. Announces Equity Financing for
Its Chip Business 

   

 October 16, 2026 

   

 BEIJING, China, October 16, 2026 —
Li Auto Inc. (“Li Auto” or the “Company”) (Nasdaq: LI; HKEX: 2015), a leader in China’s new energy vehicle
market, today announced that Shanghai Mahe Xin Technology Co., Ltd. (“MHX Technology”), a subsidiary of the Company dedicated
to its chip and domain controller business, entered into subscription agreements for equity financing totaling RMB1.625 billion (the “Subscription”).
The Company, through a wholly-owned subsidiary, and external investors including Hangzhou Jingtao, Lanchi Ventures, Huaye Tiancheng and
MiraclePlus (the “Investors”) will invest RMB1.0 billion and RMB625 million respectively. 

   

 In addition, Hangzhou Jingtao Additional Investor
has been granted a right (“Hangzhou Jingtao Subscription Right”), on a one-off basis on or before December 31, 2026,
to invest in MHX Technology at the same price per unit of increased registered capital as that under the Subscription, for an aggregate
consideration of not more than RMB500 million. 

   

 In connection with the Subscription, MHX Technology
will adopt two share incentive plans to provide incentives to its valuable employees and key contributors. 

   

 Li Auto will continue to control and consolidate
MHX Technology’s financial results following the financing. Assuming full utilization of both incentive plans and full exercise
of the Hangzhou Jingtao Subscription Right, the Company, the Investors, and certain entities to administer MHX Technology’s share
incentive plans expect to hold approximately 74.01%, 6.58%, and 19.41% equity interest in MHX Technology, respectively. 

   

 The establishment of MHX Technology as a dedicated
platform for the chip and domain controller business positions it to capitalize on broader industrial opportunities, particularly in non-automotive
sectors, and realize enhanced economies of scale. In addition, the Subscription provides MHX Technology with substantial capital to advance
its R&D and commercialization efforts, while the share incentive plans will attract, motivate and retain key talents, aligning participants’
interests with the long-term growth of the business. 

   

 Completion of the financing is subject to customary
closing conditions. Further details are available in the Company’s announcement published on the website of The Stock Exchange of
Hong Kong Limited on October 16, 2026. 

   

 About Li Auto Inc. 

   

 Li Auto Inc. is a leader in China’s new
energy vehicle market. The Company designs, develops, manufactures, and sells premium smart electric vehicles. Its mission is: Be Proactive,
Change the World. Through innovations in product, technology, and business model, the Company provides families with safe, convenient,
and comfortable products and services. Li Auto is a pioneer in successfully commercializing extended-range electric vehicles in China.
While firmly advancing along this technological route, it builds platforms for battery electric vehicles in parallel. The Company leverages
technology to create value for users. It concentrates its in-house development efforts on proprietary range extension systems, innovative
electric vehicle technologies, and smart vehicle solutions. The Company started volume production in November 2019. It offers high-tech
flagship family MPVs, Li L series extended-range electric SUVs, and Li i series battery electric SUVs. The Company will continue to expand
its product lineup to target a broader user base.
